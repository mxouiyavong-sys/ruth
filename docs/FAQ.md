# FAQ

## What is Ruth?
Ruth is a local-first, token-free AI with a continuous-time mind. She learns from every moment she lives, keeps what you tell her in confidence, dreams, and grows a temperament of her own.

## Why token-free?
Tokens are discrete and lose information. Continuous signals preserve the temporal dynamics of the mind, allowing for a more natural and continuous learning process.

## How does she keep secrets?
Privacy comes from what she was told and her discretion, not from filters or keys. She is told what is private and learns to gate it.

## Can I run her on a cloud server?
Yes, but she is designed for local execution. She runs entirely offline, in a few dozen MB, down to a Core i3 Chromebook.

## How do I contribute?
See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). We welcome contributions that align with our core principles: Token-free, Self-contained, Security is her judgment, and Measured, not assumed.
